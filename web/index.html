Flapboard

Live departures and arrivals from NS, refreshed every 30 seconds. The key is free: make an account at apiportal.ns.nl, subscribe to the product Ns-App, and copy its primary key here. It is kept on this device only.

"Surprise me" picks something new at every roll: the automatic one (set "Roll all flaps"), the Roll button, and each time the screensaver starts.

Stations, airports, train types and airlines are real; the services, times, delays and gates are made up on the spot, and change during the day as on a real board. It is not live information.
